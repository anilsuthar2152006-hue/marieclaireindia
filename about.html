Marie Claire
Parisian Heritage

Born from the iconic French fashion magazine, Marie Claire Salon brings the essence of Parisian chic to the world of beauty. Since our inception, we have championed a philosophy that beauty is an expression of individuality, grounded in sophistication and effortless grace.

Our heritage is built on decades of curating trends, understanding women's desires, and translating high fashion into wearable, accessible luxury. Every service we offer is infused with this rich editorial history, ensuring our clients receive not just a treatment, but a transformative experience.

Global Excellence, Local Nuance

The Indian Branch

Bringing the Parisian standard to India meant more than just opening doors; as the Exclusive India Licensee - B2C Network LLP, it meant adapting our global expertise to celebrate Indian beauty. We have meticulously tailored our services, products, and techniques to cater to the diverse and unique needs of the Indian clientele.

Our salons across India, with presences in vibrant cities like Hyderabad, Pune, Delhi, and Chennai, serve as sanctuaries of minimalist luxury, offering an escape from the bustling city outside. Here, the precision of French styling meets the warmth of Indian hospitality.
